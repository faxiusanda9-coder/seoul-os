# Concept image provenance

`public/hero-concept.png` was created for this project with the built-in imagegen tool. It is an original concept image, not an official photo of a JYP artist.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial hero photograph for a refined K-pop fan web app, SEOUL.OS. Create a premium Korean independent music magazine cover style photograph, 16:9 landscape composition. A small anonymous group of four young adult music performers seen from behind in a modern rehearsal studio, loose dark charcoal and cream outfits, soft warm ivory sunlight through tall windows, pale sage accents, quiet cinematic atmosphere, analog grain, subtle motion in fabric. Leave the left third naturally darker and uncluttered for HTML typography overlay; subjects occupy middle/right. No recognizable celebrities, no logos, no text, no album art, no watermarks. Photographic realism and elegant editorial art direction. This is clearly concept imagery and should not resemble specific JYP artists.
